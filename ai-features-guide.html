How to Use Your Phone's Built-In AI Assistant Like a Pro

From on-device summarization to generative photo editing — get the most out of Apple Intelligence, Gemini, and Galaxy AI, and stay in control of exactly what they can see.

May 12, 2026
8 minute read
WhatPhone Editorial

Every flagship phone sold in 2026 ships with a built-in AI assistant baked into the operating system — Apple Intelligence, Google's Gemini, or Samsung's Galaxy AI. Yet most people only ever touch the headline feature (usually the camera tricks covered in our AI photography guide) and ignore the dozen smaller tools quietly saving time every day.

This guide walks through what these assistants actually do differently from a regular chatbot app, how to set them up properly, and — just as importantly — how to control what personal data they're allowed to touch. Step 1: Understand What "On-Device AI" Actually Means

The biggest shift in phone AI since 2024 isn't the features themselves — it's where the processing happens. Modern flagship chips (Apple's A17/A18 Neural Engine, Google's Tensor, Qualcomm's Snapdragon Elite with its dedicated NPU) can run compact AI models directly on the silicon, without sending anything to a server.

That matters for two reasons: speed (no round-trip to a data center) and privacy (your message drafts, photos, and notifications never have to leave the device for routine tasks). When a request is too complex for the on-device model, your phone can escalate it to a larger cloud model — Apple calls this Private Cloud Compute, Google routes it through Gemini's cloud tier — typically with encryption and a no-retention policy specifically for that request.

Step 2: Set Up and Personalize Your Assistant

On iPhone (Apple Intelligence)

1

Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri

Toggle Apple Intelligence on. It downloads a set of on-device models (a few gigabytes) in the background over Wi-Fi, so give it 10–15 minutes on your first setup before features become available.

2

Enable Personal Context

This lets Siri and Apple Intelligence reference your emails, messages, and calendar to answer questions like "what time is my flight" without you switching apps. It's opt-in and processed on-device.

On Android (Gemini / Galaxy AI)

1

Set Gemini as your default assistant

Settings → Apps → Default apps → Digital assistant app, or open the Gemini app directly and follow the setup prompt. On Samsung, Galaxy AI features are built into native apps and don't require a separate default-assistant switch.

2

Turn on Gemini Live and Circle to Search

Gemini Live enables real-time conversational voice mode; Circle to Search (Settings → Search → Circle to Search) lets you circle anything on screen — text, an object, a landmark — to search or ask about it instantly.

Step 3: Use AI Writing and Summarization Tools System-Wide

These tools work almost anywhere you can type or read text — Mail, Messages, Notes, and most third-party apps that support the system text field.

  • Rewrite / Tone shift — Highlight any text and choose Friendly, Professional, or Concise to instantly rephrase it.
  • Summarize — Long email threads, web articles, and PDF documents can be condensed to a few bullet points from the share sheet or a right-click menu.
  • Notification summaries — Group chats and busy apps get rolled into a one-line summary in the notification center instead of a wall of individual alerts.
  • Live transcription — Voice memos and phone calls (where legally permitted) can be transcribed and summarized automatically after the call ends.
✅
Pro tip: If notification summaries ever misrepresent an urgent message, you can disable summarization per-app in Settings → Notifications rather than turning the whole feature off.

Step 4: Master AI Photo and Video Editing

Generative editing tools — Apple's Clean Up, Google's Magic Editor, Samsung's Generative Edit — let you remove, move, or resize objects in a photo using AI to fill in the background convincingly.

1

Remove unwanted objects or people

Open the photo, tap the edit/cleanup tool, and brush over the object you want gone. The model reconstructs the background using surrounding pixels and similar scenes from its training data.

2

Reposition or resize a subject

Magic Editor and Generative Edit let you drag a subject to a different part of the frame — useful for fixing off-center shots — and the AI regenerates the surrounding area to match lighting and perspective.

Step 5: Control What Your AI Assistant Can Access

The convenience of an assistant that reads your messages and photos comes with a real privacy trade-off, and it's worth auditing the permissions rather than accepting the defaults.

1

Review per-app AI access

On iPhone: Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri → App Access. On Android: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Gemini apps activities. Both let you revoke access to specific apps individually.

2

Disable cloud escalation if you prefer strict on-device only

Some phones let you cap requests to on-device processing only, which limits certain advanced features but guarantees nothing ever leaves the phone.
