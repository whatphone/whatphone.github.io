Features

What This Motion Sensors Suite Includes

A full laboratory for your phone's inertial sensors — right in the browser.

Live Accelerometer Graph

Real-time X/Y/Z acceleration plot in m/s². Detect gravity, tilt, and shake intensity with a rolling 240-sample buffer.

Live Gyroscope Graph

Real-time alpha/beta/gamma rotation-rate graph in °/s. Track angular velocity on all three axes.

3D Orientation Model

A live 3D device cube that mimics your phone's real orientation using alpha, beta, and gamma angles.

Shake Detection Test

A real-time shake meter that counts discrete shake events, tracks peak acceleration and RMS, and gives an accuracy verdict.

Tilt Accuracy Test

A digital bubble level with pitch, roll, and total-tilt readouts. Confirms whether your accelerometer is level-calibrated.

Compass Calibration Check

Live compass rose with heading, cardinal direction, and accuracy indicator. Guides you through figure-8 calibration.

FAQ

Motion Sensors Suite — Common Questions

Everything you need to know about testing your device's motion sensors.

Open this page on your phone, tap "Enable Sensors", and allow motion access when prompted. The real-time graphs will start plotting accelerometer and gyroscope data as you move, tilt, or shake your device. Use the shake and tilt tests to verify accuracy, and the compass calibration check to confirm heading.

Most browsers require explicit permission for motion sensors on non-secure origins or newer iOS versions. Make sure you are on HTTPS, tap "Enable Sensors", and grant the permission prompt. Some desktop browsers without motion hardware will not produce data — that is normal.

The accelerometer measures linear acceleration including gravity, so it can detect tilt and shake. The gyroscope measures the rate of rotation around each axis, so it tracks angular velocity. Together they enable full motion tracking. This suite shows both graphs live.

Place the device on a flat, level surface and check the tilt readout. If it does not show roughly 0° on pitch and roll, move the phone in a figure-8 pattern several times. For the compass, rotate the phone slowly around each axis. Then re-open the tilt test to verify the calibration.
