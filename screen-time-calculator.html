How to use the screen time calculator

  1. Enter your daily average. Type your daily screen time in hours or tap a preset. Your phone's Screen Time or Digital Wellbeing report shows the number.
  2. Add your age details. Enter your age, the age you got a smartphone and the life expectancy you want to plan with.
  3. Set sleep and a goal. Your sleep sets the waking hours, and the goal shows how much time you would reclaim by cutting back.
  4. Read the result. See hours per week and year, how much of your waking day your phone takes, and your lifetime total.

How much of your life do you spend on your phone?

Small daily numbers add up quickly. Five hours a day is about 1,825 hours a year, which is roughly 76 full days. This screen time calculator multiplies your daily average across the years you have left and compares it with your waking hours, so the figure reflects the time you are actually awake.

Years of life versus years of waking life

Most people sleep around a third of their lives, so a phone habit is better measured against waking hours. Five hours a day is about 31% of a 16-hour day, which is why the headline number is in years of waking life.

Reduce the number without deleting everything

Set the goal field to a realistic target and see the time you would get back. Our phone screen time guide covers app limits, grayscale and notification changes that make cutting back stick.
