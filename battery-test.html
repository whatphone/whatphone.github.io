Why It Matters

The Battery Test Built for Every Device

A fast, browser-based battery test that works on phones, tablets, and laptops — with real-time monitoring and a live drain-rate chart.

Live Battery Level

See your exact battery percentage update in real time, with a large visual gauge that changes color as it drops from green to amber to red.

Charging Detection

Instantly detect if your device is charging, see the estimated time to full charge, and confirm your charger is delivering power correctly.

Drain Rate Analysis

Measures how fast your battery drops over time and charts it live. High drain rates often signal background apps, aging cells, or heavy screen use.

Health Estimate

Combines level stability, drain rate, and voltage readings to give you a rough health score — useful for checking a used phone before buying.

Live Drain Chart

Watch your battery percentage plotted on a live chart over a 5-minute window so you can spot sudden drops or unusually fast drain instantly.

100% Private, Runs Locally

Everything runs client-side using the standard Battery Status API. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or shared — closing the tab clears all data.

The Process

How to Test Your Battery Online

Four steps to check your battery level, health, and drain rate — all without installing anything.

Start the Test

Tap "Start Battery Test". Your browser reads your battery through the Battery Status API — no permission popup required on supported browsers.

View Live Data

Your current level, charging status, and time estimates appear immediately — with a large visual gauge at the top of the screen.

Watch the Drain

Keep the tab open for a few minutes. The live chart plots your level over time and calculates an accurate hourly drain rate.

Review Results

The sidebar shows all six diagnostic checks — API access, level readability, charging detection, drain rate, health estimate, and voltage stability.

Everything you need to know about testing your phone or laptop battery online.

Open this page on your phone or laptop browser, tap "Start Battery Test", and allow the browser to access battery information when prompted. The live test shows your battery level, charging state, drain rate, and estimated health — no app or download required.

The test measures your battery level, charging state, discharge rate, and voltage to estimate overall health. Phone batteries change level slowly — usually 1% per few minutes when idle. The test tracks changes in real time and calculates the drain rate over a monitoring window. For best results, run the test for a few minutes with the screen on.

A typical modern smartphone drains around 5-10% per hour during light use, 10-20% per hour during active use, and 20%+ per hour during heavy gaming or video streaming. Rates significantly above these may indicate an aging battery or a power-hungry app.
