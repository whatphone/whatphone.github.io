Tips

Tips for links that work

  • Always include the country code. A local number without it opens the wrong chat or an error page.
  • Test every link on a phone before printing it or putting it in an ad.
  • Keep the pre-filled message short and specific, such as the product name, so you know what the customer wants.
  • Use a separate link or message for each campaign so you can see which one brings chats.
  • Only generate links for people who have agreed to be contacted. Follow WhatsApp's terms and local messaging rules.
  • Do not post a private number in public places unless you want people to message it.
