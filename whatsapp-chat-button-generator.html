Tips

Tips for a button that gets more chats

  • Use the number you actually monitor. Test the link from a second phone before you publish.
  • Keep the pre-filled message short and specific, for example the product name, so you can reply with context.
  • Do not auto-open the popup instantly. A delay of 10 to 20 seconds is less intrusive and works better on mobile.
  • Put the button on the side that does not cover your cookie banner or other floating buttons.
  • Mention your reply hours near the button so visitors know when to expect an answer.
  • Always follow your local rules for contacting people. Only message those who start a chat with you.
