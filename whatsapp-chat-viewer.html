Step by step

How to use the WhatsApp Chat Export Viewer & Stats Reader

From WhatsApp export to chat statistics in six steps.

  1. Export the chat from WhatsApp.Open the chat, use the export option and choose Without media. This creates a small .txt file.
  2. Save the file on your device.Send it to yourself by email or save it to Files, Drive or Downloads so you can find it.
  3. Open it in this tool.Tap Choose .txt file or drag the file onto the box. Parsing takes a second or two, even for large chats.
  4. Read the summary.See total messages, active days, your busiest day and hour, and how many photos, links and emojis were shared.
  5. Explore the charts.Compare who talks more, when you chat and how activity changed month by month, plus top words and emojis.
  6. Search the conversation.Filter by person or date range and search for any word, then export the stats as a CSV file.

About this tool

A WhatsApp export is just a long text file. This tool turns it into something you can read, search and understand.

What the statistics mean

StatisticHow it is calculated
MessagesEvery message with a sender. System notices are counted separately.
Median replyTime from the other person's message to your reply, counted only when under 6 hours.
Started chatsMessages sent after a gap of more than 6 hours, or the very first message.
StreakThe longest run of consecutive days with at least one message.

Tips

Tips for accurate results

  • Export without media. The text file stays small and loads faster, and media is not needed for statistics.
  • If the dates look wrong, change the date format setting. It matters only when every date in the chat is 12 or lower.
  • For group chats with many members, use the Person filter to read one member's messages at a time.
  • Check the top words list with common words hidden to quickly see what the chat is about.
  • Remember that your exported chat contains private messages. Delete the file from shared folders when you are done.
  • Very large chats with hundreds of thousands of messages can take a few seconds on older phones, so keep the tab open until parsing finishes.
