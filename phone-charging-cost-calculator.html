How to use the phone charging cost calculator

  1. Enter your phone battery. Use the mAh from the spec sheet or tap a preset.
  2. Set how much you charge. Enter the share of the battery you refill per day. 80% is a typical daily top-up, and 100% is one full charge a day.
  3. Add your electricity price. Type the price per kWh (unit) from your bill and your currency symbol.
  4. Read the cost. See the cost per charge, per month, per year and over three years.

How much does it cost to charge a phone?

Less than most people expect. A typical phone battery holds roughly 17 watt-hours. After charger losses a full charge draws about 23 Wh from the wall, which is 0.023 kWh. At 0.15 per kWh that is about a third of a cent per charge. Over a year of daily charging it comes to roughly one unit of currency.

What changes the cost

Three things matter: your electricity price, battery size and charger efficiency. Fast charging loses a little more energy as heat, but the difference is tiny. Read our charging cost guide for ways to save and for how a phone compares with other devices.
