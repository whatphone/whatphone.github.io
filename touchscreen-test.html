Why It Matters

A Touchscreen Test That Checks Everything

One free tool to check dead zones, multi-touch, swipe accuracy, and response time — built for phones, tablets, and touch laptops.

Dead Zone Grid Test

Drag across a 48-cell grid covering the full screen. Any cell that won't light up green is a likely dead spot on your digitizer.

Multi-Touch Test

Place up to 10 fingers on screen at once and watch each one tracked live, confirming your screen's true multi-touch limit.

Swipe & Gesture Test

Swipe in any direction and this touchscreen test reports the detected direction, distance, and gesture speed instantly.

Response Time Readout

Troubleshooting

Common Touchscreen Problems This Test Reveals

If a check fails above, here's what it usually means.

Dark Cells That Won't Clear

A cell that never turns green even after repeated dragging points to a physical dead zone on the digitizer or a cracked layer underneath.

Multi-Touch Stops at One Finger

If only a single touch point ever appears, your screen's max touch points value may be capped low, or a screen protector is interfering with sensing.

Swipes Detected in the Wrong Direction

Inconsistent swipe results usually mean digitizer noise or interference — try cleaning the screen and removing any case near the edges.

High Response Time

Response times consistently above 150ms can indicate background CPU load, an aging battery throttling performance, or a thick screen protector.

Touches Landing Off-Target

A consistent offset between where you tap and where the touch registers points to a digitizer calibration issue, often after a drop or repair.

No Touch Detected At All

If the "Touch Support" check fails immediately, your device or browser may not expose touch events — try a different browser or device.
