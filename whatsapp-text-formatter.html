Tips

Tips for clear WhatsApp messages

  • Do not leave a space between the symbol and your text. Write *sale*, not * sale *.
  • Use bold for one key phrase per paragraph. Too much bold makes nothing stand out.
  • Put each list item on its own line starting with a dash and a space, or a number and a period.
  • Use fancy fonts for short names or headings only. Long paragraphs in styled Unicode are hard to read and are not searchable.
  • Check the message on a phone before you broadcast it, since display can differ slightly between apps and versions.
  • Keep important details such as phone numbers and prices in normal text so they stay easy to copy.
