iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro: Everything Apple Changed in 2026

Price, specs and availability of Apple's first foldable and the new Pro models, with the latest on shortages and software.

November 12, 2026
5 minute read
WhatPhone Editorial

Apple's 2026 iPhone launch broke with tradition in two ways. It introduced its first foldable, the iPhone Duo, and it skipped the standard iPhone 18 for now, putting the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max in the spotlight. This article covers what changed, what it costs and what has happened since the phones went on sale. Details are accurate as of early November 2026.

iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max: What Changed

The Pro models look much like last year's phones, but the changes inside are significant.

  • A20 Pro chip. Built on TSMC's 2-nanometer process with a 6-core CPU, 7-core GPU and 32-core Neural Engine. Apple says sustained performance is up to 40% faster than the A19 Pro, helped by a vapor chamber cooling system three times larger than last year's.
  • Variable aperture camera. The 48 MP main camera can change its aperture between f/1.48 and f/4.0, a first for iPhone. It gives more control over depth of field and light. All three rear cameras are 48 MP, including a 100 mm periscope telephoto at 4x optical zoom, and Apple describes 8x zoom as optical quality.
  • Battery. Apple says the iPhone 18 Pro matches last year's Pro Max with up to 24 hours of typical use, while the Pro Max reaches up to 30 hours, its biggest generational jump. Apple also says the Pro can charge to 50% in 15 minutes.
  • Apple's C2 modem. Apple's own cellular modem replaces the Qualcomm modem used in the previous Pro generation, according to launch coverage.
💡
Note: Battery and performance figures are Apple's own claims. Independent testing can differ, especially for real-world battery life.

iPhone Duo: Apple's First Foldable

The iPhone Duo is a book-style foldable. It has a 7.6-inch inner display (7.58 inches measured as a rectangle with rounded corners) and a 5.4-inch outer display. It is 5.2 mm thin when unfolded, weighs 254 grams and uses a titanium frame and hinge. It runs the same A20 Pro chip as the Pro phones and carries 12 GB of RAM, according to GSMArena's specifications.

Shortages, Display Reports and the App Gap

The Duo has been hard to buy. It sold out within hours of pre-orders opening, and delivery estimates slipped into late January 2027. On Apple's earnings call on November 2, its first under CEO John Ternus, executives said the Duo is supply constrained and that they cannot predict when supply and demand will balance. Apple also reported a record September quarter with $114.5 billion in revenue and iPhone revenue of $57.6 billion, which did not include any Duo sales because the phone went on sale after the quarter ended.

Two other issues have appeared. Apple said it was aware of an issue affecting the inner display of some units and told customers to contact AppleCare. Ternus said a very small percentage of units had been returned and that Apple is offering replacements. Early reviews also criticized the lack of apps made for the larger screen, and on November 6 Apple said more than 100,000 Duo-optimized apps are now on the App Store, joining apps such as Netflix, YouTube, Instagram and WhatsApp.
