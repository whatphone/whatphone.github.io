Troubleshooting

Common Camera Problems This Test Reveals

If a check fails above, here's what it usually means.

🚫

Camera Access Denied

Usually a browser permission was blocked. Check your site settings and allow camera access, then reload this camera test page.

🌀

Blurry or Out-of-Focus Feed

Could be a dirty lens, a stuck autofocus motor, or low light. Wipe the lens and re-run the test in a brighter area.

🐢

Low Frame Rate

If the FPS readout stays low, another app may be using the camera in the background, or your device may be throttling performance.

🔄

Switch Camera Not Working

Some devices only expose one camera to the browser. Re-run the test and check the "Cameras Found" count in the sidebar.

💡

Torch / Flash Unsupported

Torch control through the browser only works on select Android devices. iOS Safari currently doesn't expose torch access to web apps.

📉

Resolution Below 720p

A failed resolution check can mean a lower-end camera sensor, a degraded webcam, or the browser falling back to a reduced stream.
