Why Use Dynamic Timestamps

End "What Time Is That in My Timezone?" Forever

Writing "Event starts at 8 PM EST" forces half your Discord server to do mental timezone math—and miss the start.

Discord's native timestamp syntax uses standard Unix Epoch time (the number of seconds elapsed since January 1, 1970 UTC). Because Discord renders <t:UNIX:FLAG> natively on iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and web clients, every member sees the date and time formatted in their own device's language, 12/24-hour clock preference, and local timezone.

Tips

Tips for Clean Discord Timestamp Formatting

  • Combine :F and :R together (e.g., <t:1735689600:F> (<t:1735689600:R>)) so members see both the calendar date and a live countdown.
  • Always check uppercase vs. lowercase flags: :d gives numeric dates while :D spells out the month name.
  • Discord timestamps work inside regular text, bold (**<t:…>**), and blockquotes, but will not render inside inline `code blocks`.
  • On mobile Discord, tapping a rendered timestamp displays a native tooltip showing the full date and exact relative time.
  • If you omit the flag (<t:1735689600>), Discord defaults to the :f Short Date/Time style.
